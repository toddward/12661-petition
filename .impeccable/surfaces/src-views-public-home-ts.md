# Surface: landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Mobile-first: most visitors arrive from the QR code on a paper mailer, on a phone.
Audience and job: one adult per Victory Lakes household; understand the ask, trust that neighbors run it, sign or pledge in about two minutes.
After the first screen they must believe: neighbors run this (not the county, VDOT or the HOA); children's safety on the loop is at stake; there is a real county process (HOA board, speed study, petition at 75% of occupied households, county approval); it takes two minutes.
Must not feel: official, alarmist, cute. Polished is welcome.
Proof on hand: general claims only. Neighbor accounts may come later. Any map is illustrative and must say so: the county defines the real petition area.
Related surfaces inherit this world: /sign and /admin (Operate, code-led), /faq and /privacy (Read), /thanks, /withdraw, print view.
